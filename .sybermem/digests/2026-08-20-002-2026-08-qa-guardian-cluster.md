## Phase Scope

QA Guardian runtime hardening across August 2026: investigation/plan/QA gate core, artifact and state persistence, role/actor separation, SDK session continuity, Windows/ops reliability, and review remediation of previously-blocking runtime defects.

## Core Conclusions

- Evidence-first pipeline: specialist investigation -> dossier/plan artifacts (atomic, revision-bound) -> plan gate (legacy/shadow/enforced) -> independent QA verdict -> GATE_2_WAIT PR.
- Enforced mode is the safe default: only a dossier-backed, decision-complete LOW plan runs autonomously; HIGH or uncertain plans stop at Gate 1 for human approval.
- Actor routing is structural: only a trusted human authorizer may authorize; QA and fixer are read-only / code-write only; Supervisor owns QA verdict comments and PR creation.
- N=1 concurrency, lock heartbeat, and idempotent comment/state handling cover the whole critical section (investigation + fixer + QA + PR).
